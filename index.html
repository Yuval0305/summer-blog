  3. The Hunger Games
    Title: The Hunger Games
    Author: Suzanne Collins
    Age rating: 12+ some violence and mature themes
    Genre: Dystopian, YA, Adventure, Fiction, coming of age
    Length: 374- 384 pages for book 1 (the rest are similar)

    Summary:
    I'm not going to summarize the whole series because it would take too long (I know that's the whole point of tldr, but oh well. Maybe I'll do a blog entry about it later). The Hunger Games takes place in Panem, a futuristic North America after some disaster has befallen Earth. It follows Katniss Everdeen, a girl from District 12 (the poorest district), as she sacrifices herself to enter the Hunger Games, an IRL fight-to-the-death entertainment show for the rich. She manages to survive and begins to understand that she must fight to save her people. With her newfound publicity, she becomes the leader of a rebellion.

    Should you spend your time on this?
    Yes! Many have probably heard of The Hunger Games series, and if you haven't read it, you're missing out. I swear this isn't just a biased opinion because it's one of my favourite series. It's one of the few, if not the only, books I have never heard a negative comment about. Suzanne Collins uses simple yet compelling language to draw in the reader and keep you engaged all the way through. The distinct differences between the districts and the Capitol, and the similarities between them and our world, make you question reality and what we believe in. If you want a gripping series with strong worldbuilding and a thoughtful message, this is a great choice.
