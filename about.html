I'm a bookworm (duhhh). I'm also a world-renowned dancer, just kidding. But I seriously do love to dance. It's my way to express myself. When I dance, I feel happier and connected to the world. My favourite style is lyrical, a mix of ballet and jazz in which the dancers tell a story or perform a deeply emotional piece. I think it’s one of the most beautiful styles to watch, and it is quite easy for the audience to understand. Dancing it lets you pour out all your emotions and move freely. I hope this will inspire you to watch some lyrical, or maybe even to dance yourself.
